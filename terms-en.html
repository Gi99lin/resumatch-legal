In short

The bot scores and rewrites resumes using artificial intelligence. It's help preparing documents, not a guarantee of employment. The result may contain errors — reread it before sending it to an employer. Paid features are refunded in money only in case of a technical failure.

1. What this service is

ResuMatch is a Telegram bot that analyzes resumes, scores them against formal ATS criteria, gives improvement tips, rewrites the text for hh.ru, drafts cover letters for a specific job posting, and matches suitable job openings, including through a regular digest of new matches. All results are produced by a language model, not a human.

The service is operated by an individual, owner of the Telegram account @Gigglin. You can reach the Operator on Telegram or by email at support@gigglin.tech.
