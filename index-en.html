ResuMatch

A Telegram bot for resume analysis and improvement

ResuMatch is a Telegram bot for resumes and job search: it scores your resume against ATS criteria, explains its weak points, rewrites the text for hh.ru, drafts cover letters for a specific job posting, and matches suitable openings. It runs inside Telegram: @GigglinReviewbot.

How it works

  1. Upload your resume as PDF, DOCX or DOC — the first analysis is free.
  2. Get an ATS score, a breakdown of strengths and weaknesses, and free improvement tips.
  3. If you want, order a paid rewrite of your resume for hh.ru, a cover letter for a job posting, or a "full polish" bundling both.
  4. Get matching job openings and, if you like, subscribe to a regular digest of new matches.

There's a referral program (a free cover letter for inviting a friend) and a PRO subscription — unlimited use of every paid action.
